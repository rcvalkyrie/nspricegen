NetSuite Calculator Setup

⚡ Calculator — Aiza Dalmacion (PM) [designer]

📖 How to Setup & Use

  1. Initial Setup: Select your name, team, and paste the field path (preserves case-sensitivity). Drag the orange button directly to your browser's Bookmarks Bar.
  2. Opening in NetSuite: Open any NetSuite Opportunity or Quote record, then click your ⚡ Calculator bookmark.
  3. Calculating Prices: Select or type plaque sizes and proof source. The tool auto-detects location fees (Canada / AK / HI), EVG / Partnership fees, and oversized dimensions.
  4. Applying Values: Click Apply to NetSuite to automatically fill in the price, designer name, proof source, and publication link path.
